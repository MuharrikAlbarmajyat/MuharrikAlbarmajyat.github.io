KeyTempo privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026. Publisher: Muharrik Albarmajyat.

KeyTempo is a Windows app that presses the keys you choose, on a schedule you choose, in the app you point it at. It runs on your PC. It has no account, no advertising, and no analytics.

What stays on your PC

KeyTempo stores its settings in its private app folder on this PC. That includes your profiles (the name, the key or mouse button, the timing, the repeat limit, and the target program name or window title you typed), the start/stop hotkey, theme, window position, and whether you asked it to start with Windows or show the status overlay. While a key is held down, it also keeps a short marker so a later launch can release that key if the app was closed unexpectedly. The marker is cleared when the key is released.

None of this is sent to the publisher. Uninstalling KeyTempo removes it.

Keys

The keys KeyTempo sends go to a window on this same PC, through Windows. KeyTempo does not record what you type in other programs, and it does not upload keystrokes. When you use “record” to choose a key or a hotkey, it watches only that one press, and only while that button is waiting, and it saves the choice in the profile on this PC.

Files you import or export

Import and export use the Windows file picker. A profile file is read or written only where you choose. KeyTempo does not scan your disks.

Network

KeyTempo does not contain analytics, crash reporting, or advertising. It contacts the network only when you click Privacy policy (this page) or Rate KeyTempo (the Microsoft Store review page). Windows and the Microsoft Store handle purchase, install, and updates under their own terms. Windows may also record an application crash under your Windows error-reporting settings. That is Windows, not a report KeyTempo sends.

Start with Windows

Starting at sign-in is off until you turn it on in KeyTempo’s settings. You can turn it off there, or in Windows Settings under Apps and Startup.

Children

KeyTempo is not directed at children and does not ask for anyone’s age or identity.

Changes

If this policy changes, the page and the effective date will be updated. The app does not start collecting data under a new policy on its own.
